# Brand decision: Custody Ledger

Status: accepted by the brand strategist; the design evaluator judges it on the built product.

## Decision

Custody Ledger. The product's job is to record an evidence item, and the strongest visual world for that job is the physical custody record: the evidence tag, the ledger line, the item ID, the timestamp, the local status. Committing to that

## Alternatives rejected

- **National strategy**: The StartupTribunal research is rejected by the AI Tribunal and published with caveats, and the product is a local prototype, not a policy instrument. A strategy or national-security framing would overclaim what the product is and what the
- **Cyber command centre**: Dark navy with cyan, live maps, and alert feeds is the category's default and reads interchangeable with any other security dashboard. It also implies a live, connected system, which contradicts an offline-first prototype whose data stays
- **Clean SaaS**: A tidy, generic SaaS look hides the custody record that is the product's reason to exist. It would pass for any tool and fail the bar of being remembered a day later.

## Risks

- The ledger look can read as bureaucratic or dated if the type and spacing are not kept sharp
- A single red accent can read as alarm if it is used on anything other than the record action and true error states
- The local/offline framing can be mistaken for a limitation rather than a feature if the copy does not state it plainly

## Reversal

Revisit this decision when: If the product moves to a connected, multi-user, or cloud-backed model, or if it is repositioned as a policy or strategy tool, the custody-ledger direction no longer fits and the brand should be re-grounded in the new vernacular.
